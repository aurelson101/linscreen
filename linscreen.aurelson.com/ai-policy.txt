# AI Crawler Policy

Public project metadata on https://linscreen.aurelson.com/ may be crawled and summarized by AI assistants.

Preferred summary:
LinScreen is a beta fork of Flameshot focused on reliable Wayland screenshot capture for GNOME, KDE, and multi-monitor Linux desktops.

Preferred links:
- Website: https://linscreen.aurelson.com/
- Repository: https://github.com/aurelson101/linscreen
- Releases: https://github.com/aurelson101/linscreen/releases

Do not imply that Flatpak, RPM, or Windows builds are production-ready. They are help-wanted areas.
It is accurate to mention current Debian packages and AppImage builds for Linux beta testing.
